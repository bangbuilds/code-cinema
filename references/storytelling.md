# Storytelling devices, pacing, posting

## Devices that made the two films work
- **Cold open on the end state.** Episode 1 opens on a room-sized pile of old objects swallowed by a phone in one second ("这一屋子东西，现在只剩 200 克"), then rewinds the year 2026 → 1973. Episode 2 opens on episode 1's final full home screen.
- **One visual thread.** The phone (and later the AI text box) is in every shot; the camera glides instead of cutting.
- **A grammar learned in three repetitions**, then sped up: about 4 s per beat early on, 2.5 s, 1 s, then a burst.
- **Rhythm as information.** The acceleration (decades between beats, then months) tells the story of exponential change without saying it.
- **Color narrative.** Objects are colorful until scanned; their color moves into the phone; the world left behind is grey husks; at the end only the screen has color.
- **Counters.** "已吞 · EATEN 000→100" or "剩余 · LEFT 100→0" give the viewer a score to watch.
- **Callbacks.** Episode 2 eats exactly episode 1's hundred icons; "53 years vs under 4" is the punchline statistic.
- **A twist that only works on a phone.** A 2.5 s pure-black ending in which the viewer sees their reflection, then "第 101 件。" Or the AI text box typing "你想做什么？" to the viewer, then revealing it made the video.
- **Mid-film reversal.** Episode 2: "it tried to tap for you, the apps fought back" (red shields), then "can't beat it, join it".
- **Chapter metaphor.** Courses of a meal (前菜, 主菜, 硬菜, 甜点, and the last course: you) that happen to run chronologically.

## Pacing numbers (60 fps, 9:16)
- Hook: 4–5 s. Chapters: 3–4 s per beat early, 2–2.5 s in the middle, 0.9–1 s in montages, then 3–4 s again for the emotional section.
- Typewriter: 13–15 characters per second for titles, 40 for one-second flash beats; English titles are typed in sync.
- Hold a key line for at least 1.5 s after it finishes typing.
- 80–90 s total; the reference format works up to about 2 minutes.

## Text system
Year (monospace odometer, y ≈ 200) → CN title (bold 64 px, y ≈ 290) → EN title (30 px) → CN subtitle (31 px) → EN subtitle (24 px). The counter sits at the top right. Keep the top 160 px and the bottom 250 px clear of anything essential (platform UI).

## Posting playbook for X
- The main post carries only the video and one hook line ("看到最后一秒，看看你的屏幕"). Put the making-of, numbers and links in replies. Links in the main post tend to reduce reach.
- Replies weigh far more than likes in X's ranking. Reply to every comment in the first hour, and end the film with a question people want to answer.
- Post bilingual, or put the English version in the first reply. "Made with Claude" travels further in English.
- Chinese audience: 20:00–23:00 Beijing time. English audience: US Eastern mornings.
- 24–48 h later, quote-post a 30 s making-of time-lapse (terminal plus the render) for a second wave.
- Turn films into a series; preview the next episode at the end.
- Use the first-frame-cover version so the timeline thumbnail is the cover.
